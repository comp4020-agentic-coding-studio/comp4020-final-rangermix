## Context

The forces at play: what problem needs a decision, what constrains it (the
brief, the deadline, the stack, the data), and what we know or don't.

## Options

Each option considered, with what it would give and what it would cost.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences, stated actively ("We will ...").

## Consequences

What this costs and what it commits us to, good and bad. What becomes easier,
what becomes harder, and what would make us revisit it. Link the commits that
carry it out.
