Lofi*, each released under the
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

each one came from, out of courtesy and so anyone reading the repo can
check it. Each track's own page on the Free Music Archive was checked on

The files here aren't the originals: `scripts/music.ts` re-encoded each
once, from the 320 kbps MP3 on its page, to 128 kbps stereo at −16 LUFS
integrated loudness, so they're smaller and none is louder than another.
`manifest.json` records each file's hash and the loudness measured on it.
